Nintendo has a wide range of developers under its belt, whether as its own studios or as close partners.

Nintendo Entertainment Planning & Development​
Nintendo's main development force is the Entertainment Planning & Development Division. It is the conglomeration of Nintendo's Entertainment Analysis & Development Division and Software Planning & Development and formed in 2015, occupying Nintendo's R&D plants in Kyoto & Tokyo. It consists of over 800 developers that range from pioneers of the original Famicom to fresh recruits that will lead the games of the future. The internal R&D developers primarily work on Nintendo's largest franchises such as The Legend of Zelda, Animal Crossing and Mario as well as Nintendo's major series of the future like Splatoon and Ring Fit Adventure.

Retro Studios
Retro Studios Inc. is an American video game developer and subsidiary of Nintendo based in Austin, Texas. Retro was founded on September 21, 1998 as an alliance between Nintendo and former Iguana Entertainment founder Jeff Spangenberg, hoping to create titles for the then-upcoming GameCube aiming at an older demographic. The studio has established itself as a powerhouse with the Metroid Prime series and their revival of Donkey Kong Country.

President - Michael Kelbaugh
Releases - Donkey Kong Country Returns, Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze, Metroid Prime Remastered, Metroid Prime 4
Current - TBA

Nintendo Cube
Nintendo Cube (formerly NDcube) is a subsidiary originally established as a collaboration between Nintendo and Dentsu. The studio had a resurgence after Hudson Soft was absorbed by Konami so, the Mario Party team joined the studio with a former Hudson Soft president leading the studio. The studio has done various minigame compilations such as Wii Party, the Mario Party series starting with 9 and Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics.

President - Shuichiro Nishiya
Releases - Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp, Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics, Mario Party Superstars, Everybody 1-2 Switch, Nintendo Switch 2 Welcome Tour, Super Mario Party Jamboree Switch 2 Edition
Current - TBA

1-UP Studio
1-Up Studio is a game development studio founded in 2000 as Brownie Brown made of developers who worked on the Mana series. They collaborated with other publishers on a few titles in that early time including some Mana titles and Fantasy Life. In 2013, the studio was integrated fully as codevelopment studio for Nintendo with several of the original developers leaving to form Brownies. Since then, they have assisted in the design of 3D Mario games and supported games like Animal Crossing: New Horizons.

President - Gen Kadoi
Releases - Magical Vacation, Ring Fit Adventure, Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Super Mario 3D All-Stars, Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury, Donkey Kong Bananza
Current - Mario Kart World, SMB Wonder - Meetup in Bellabel Park

Next Level Games
Next Level Games is a game development studio founded in 2002 in Vancouver, Canada from former EA Black Box staff. Nintendo acquired the studio in 2021. Next Level is best known for their work with Nintendo including their work on the Luigi's Mansion series.

President - Douglas Tronsgard (former), Alex MacFarlane (Managing Director)
Releases - Punch-Out!!, Metroid Prime: Federation Force, Luigi's Mansion 3, Mario Strikers: Battle League, Metroid Prime 4 (support)
Current - TBA

Nintendo Software Technology
The studio was established in 1998 as a collaboration with the university, DigiPen Institute of Technology. The studio is largely taken from DigiPen graduatates and creates mostly smaller titles. More recently, they have assisted EPD in porting titles to new hardware.

President - Shigeki Yamashiro
Releases - Mario vs. Donkey Kong series, Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS, Snipperclips, Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (3DS/ Switch), Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury, Super Mario Galaxy 1 + 2
Current - F-Zero 99

Nintendo European Research & Development
Nintendo European Research & Development (NERD) is a European subsidiary based in Paris, France. It was formerly known as Mobiclip. It specializes in hardware middleware and classic game emulation.

Managing Director - Alexandre Delattre
Projects - Nintendo DS Virtual Console on Wii U, Wii-DL software on Wii U, "Super-stable 3D" on New Nintendo 3DS, NES & SNES Classic, Labo VR Kit, Super Mario 3D All-Stars (Sunshine/Galaxy emulation), Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit, Brain Training for Nintendo Switch (deep learning middleware for RPS game), Ring Fit Adventure (heart rate IR detection), Pikmin 1 + 2
Current - Nintendo Classics (ignoring Sega)

iQue
iQue is a Chinese subsidiary based in Suzhou, China. They used to distribute Nintendo products in China such as the iQue Player. The brand was dropped in 2018 when, Tencent became the official distributor. As of 2017, iQue's main role is localization for China. They have recently hired developers to help with Nintendo EPD games and their first project is work on Super Mario 3D All-Stars. They have also done some earlier emulation work with the NES emulator on the 3DS and the N64 emulator on the Wii U.

SRD
SRD (Systems Research and Development) is a longtime partner of Nintendo going back to the Famicom. They have worked with Nintendo on programming on major titles from the original Super Mario Bros. to modern titles such as Tears of the Kingdom. Nintendo acquired them in 2022. They are also involved with Server Development across the company's titles.

Nintendo Pictures
Nintendo Pictures (formerly Dynamo Pictures) is Nintendo's animation studio that had formerly worked on CG and Motion Capture for various anime and productions. For Nintendo, they have worked on the Pikmin Short Movies and the short film "Close to You". Nintendo acquired them in 2022. Since then, they have been credited in both the Super Mario Movie, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom and Super Mario Bros. Wonder. Additionally, they still provide motion capture and CG on some series like Precure

Shiver Entertainment
Shiver Entertainment is a developer based in Miami, Florida established in 2012 by former Electronic Arts executives, John Schappert and Jon Osvald. They have not worked directly with Nintendo prior to their acquisition but, they worked on several high-profile titles ported to the Nintendo Switch. In 2021, they were acquired by the Embracer Group for Saber Interactive. Nintendo acquired the developer from Embracer in May 2024. Since the acquisition, they have worked on WWE 2K25 for the Nintendo Switch 2.

Nintendo Studios Singapore
Nintendo Studios Singapore is a developer acquired from Bandai Namco in 2026. It was established in 2013, where it provided support work for various Bandai Namco Studios titles over the years including Mario Sports Superstars, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Splatoon 3 and Kirby Air Riders. Its current CEO is Makoto Ishii.
Releases - Splatoon 3, Mario Kart World, Kirby Air Riders, Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream, Splatoon Raiders
